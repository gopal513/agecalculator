Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate age?

To calculate age, subtract your year of birth from the current year. If your birthday has not occurred yet in the current calendar year, subtract one year from that total. Our exact age calculator processes this instantly, taking into account varying month lengths and leap years, to output your exact age in years, months, and days.

What is exact age formula?

The exact age formula calculates the elapsed time between your Date of Birth (DOB) and the target date:

Age = Target Date - Date of Birth

This is computed step-by-step: first, subtract the days; if negative, borrow days from the previous month. Next, subtract the months; if negative, borrow 12 months from the year value. Finally, subtract the years.

How many days old am I?

You can find out how many days old you are by calculating the total milliseconds elapsed between your birthdate and today, and converting that value to days. The formula is:

Total Days = Math.floor(Time Difference in Milliseconds / (1000 * 60 * 60 * 24))

Once you calculate your age using the form above, your total days and hours lived will be automatically computed and shown in the sub-metrics results section.
