Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 25, 2026

Welcome to Aura Age. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal details you share with us. This Privacy Policy details how we manage user data on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

Aura Age is a client-side calculator tool. All age calculations, dates processed, and results computed are handled entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not store, send, or save your date of birth or age metrics on our servers.

2. Cookies and Logs

We log non-personal browser data such as anonymous IP addresses, browser types, and navigation history for security purposes. Additionally, cookies are used to save user preferences, such as your theme choice (Dark or Light Mode).

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements on our website. These advertisers use technologies like cookies, DART cookies, and web beacons to serve relevant ads based on your visits to our site and other pages on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site.
  • Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

4. India Compliance & Data Integrity

This privacy charter aligns with general parameters of the Information Technology Act (IT Act), 2000 of the Republic of India. We ensure no unauthorized aggregation or distribution of confidential user details takes place on this platform.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this privacy statement, you can connect with us through our contact form available on this platform.
